# Serve, smash and volley: published reference values for a racket-mounted IMU

Scope: values read in full text or in a readable abstract/summary during this session. Where a value was only cited inside another paper, that is said. Nothing is estimated. Many PMC pages returned a bot-check page, so several primary papers (Reid 2007/2008, Elliott, Martin 2012, Chow, Kovacs & Ellenbecker, Girard, Whiteside) are NOT read and are listed under Gaps.

Key caveat: almost no source measures a racket-mounted sensor. Most report hand, arm or segment speed, or ball speed. Treat these as context, not as thresholds for the app.

Saturation of ±2000 deg/s gyros on serves: the only read source reporting arm/segment peaks (van Trigt 2025, upper arm) states that ±2000 deg/s is too small for arm movement during serves. No source gives racket-segment peak rates, so whether a racket gyro saturates on serves is not answered by the literature read.

## Gaps

- No racket-mounted IMU study found for serve, smash or volley. No published racket-sensor stroke classifier was read.
- Smash: no racket speed, angle or timing values found.
- Volley: forehand volley and block/punch volley numbers not found; only a two-handed backhand drive volley (Tai 2022).
- Racket angular velocity: no racket-segment value for any serve.
- Racket face angle at impact: not found for serve, smash or volley.
- Flat vs kick vs slice trajectory and racket-head numbers: not found in read sources (Reid et al. primary papers not accessible).
- Racket low point to impact (time): not found.
- Pages not read because of bot-check: Reid, Elliott & Alderson 2008 (MSSE, PMC2658985), Reid 2007 BJSM (only title and author read, https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/41/12/884.info), Martin et al. 2012 (serve-and-volley), Chow et al. 2003, Kovacs & Ellenbecker, Tanabe & Ito 2007 (abstract only, no numbers: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ijshs/5/0/5_0_157/_article), Gordon & Dapena (page read, no numbers: https://sportbm.sitehost.iu.edu/paper-tennis04.pdf).
- Gordon & Dapena: racket speed and phase timing are defined (MKF, horizontal, low point, contact) but no numeric values were on the page read.
